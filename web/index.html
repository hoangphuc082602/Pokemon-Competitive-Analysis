Team usage

# Pokémon (form) n Usage Win rate 95% CI vs band Lead

Unit: player-battle (one team in one completed battle); Bo3 games are not independent. One row per form: forms of a species are never summed. Forms hidden at team preview (e.g. Urshifu) are labelled “(seen in battle)” (n counts only teams where that form was sent out; a separate row from teams that named it at team preview) or “never sent out” (form unknown, not guessed); forms missing from the master data (e.g. Arceus type forms) are tagged “species level”. “vs band” compares with the selected band's own baseline, not with 50%. Win rates describe teams that brought the form — correlation, not causation.

Data: aggregate statistics computed from public Pokémon Showdown replays (snapshot date above) and game data from PokéAPI (BSD-3-Clause, © Paul Hallett and PokéAPI contributors). No usernames, replay ids or individual battles are published.
