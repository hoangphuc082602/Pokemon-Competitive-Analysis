Source code · Metric definitions

Team usage

# Pokémon (form) n Usage Win rate 95% CI vs band Lead

Unit: player-battle (one team in one completed battle); Bo3 games are not independent. One row per form: forms of a species are never summed. Forms hidden at team preview (e.g. Urshifu) are labelled “(seen in battle)” (n counts only teams where that form was sent out; a separate row from teams that named it at team preview) or “never sent out” (form unknown, not guessed); forms missing from the master data (e.g. Arceus type forms) are tagged “species level”. “vs band” compares with the selected band's own baseline, not with 50%. Win rates describe teams that brought the form — correlation, not causation. Click a Pokémon to see its partners and matchups.

Partners and matchups

Most frequent partners

Partner n teams Share Lift Win rate 95% CI vs band

Lift is co-occurrence, not synergy. Lift = P(both on a team) ÷ (P(A) × P(B)): above 1 means players bring the two together more often than chance. It says nothing about whether the pair works well; the win rate of teams with both is shown separately, against the band baseline.

Team-level matchups

Opposing team has n battles Win rate 95% CI Expected vs expected z

Matchups are team-level, not on-field. A row compares teams that brought the selected species with teams that brought the opponent; the other five Pokémon, leads and the choice of four are mixed in, so a low win rate is not proof of a counter. Expected is the log5 estimate from both teams' overall win rates (pA(1−pB) ÷ [pA(1−pB) + pB(1−pA)]), (pA: the selected species' teams in this band; pB: the opponent's teams over the whole regulation), so weak or strong opponents do not show up as matchups by themselves. Listed: the matchups furthest below and above that expectation (by z), lowest first. z is nominal: games of a best-of-three and one player's battles are not independent and many opponents are compared at once, so |z| < 4 is treated as noise and left uncoloured. In a rating band, the band is the selected species' player's.
