Team usage

# Species n Usage Win rate 95% CI vs band Lead

Unit: player-battle (one team in one completed battle); Bo3 games are not independent. Species level: forms are summed, masked forms (e.g. Urshifu) are shown at species level. “vs band” compares with the selected band's own baseline, not with 50%. Win rates describe teams that brought the species — correlation, not causation.

Data: aggregate statistics computed from public Pokémon Showdown replays (snapshot date above) and game data from PokéAPI (BSD-3-Clause, © Paul Hallett and PokéAPI contributors). No usernames, replay ids or individual battles are published.
