Team Builder

Build a team of up to six Pokémon (form, ability, nature, EVs / IVs, four moves) and keep several named teams in this browser. No account and no server: teams are stored on this device only, and a share link holds the whole team in the address itself. Open any member in the damage calculator as attacker or defender. Game data (forms, abilities, learnsets, natures) is from PokéAPI, limited to the Pokémon in the Meta Overview; moves are limited to the damaging moves the calculator knows.
