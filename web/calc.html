Damage Calculator

One attack, level 50: damage range, share of the defender's HP and a KO estimate. The numbers come from the project's own Python damage engine (python/calculations/, tested with pytest), run in your browser with Pyodide. Game data (base stats, typing, abilities, learnsets, natures, type chart) is from PokéAPI, limited to the Pokémon in the Meta Overview. Either side can be filled from a team saved in the Team builder.

Not modelled yet: spread-move reduction, critical hits, weather and terrain, burn, item and ability damage modifiers, Tera STAB (Tera Blast stays a Normal move), stat stages (boosts / drops), multi-hit and variable-power moves (listed power, one hit). Learnsets follow the selected regulation's game (PokéAPI: Scarlet / Violet or Champions); a learnset taken from another form or game is labelled under the move, and a Pokémon with none lists every move. Modelled: base damage, STAB, type chart, the 16 damage rolls, ability immunities / resistances (e.g. Levitate, Flash Fire, Thick Fat), -ate abilities, Normalize, Scrappy and Mold Breaker.

Damage range = the lowest and highest of the 16 random rolls (85–100%). KO chances count the rolls of one hit: “2HKO” means one hit takes at least half the HP, as the engine counts it, not the sum of two different rolls. This is a calculation from game mechanics, not observed battle data.
